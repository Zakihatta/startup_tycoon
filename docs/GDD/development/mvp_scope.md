# MVP Scope

## Goal
Playable prototype yang membuktikan loop dasar: eksplorasi kantor, interaksi, startup management, product development, employee contribution, penjualan, dan persistence.

## Included
- First-person & Third-person movement and camera (toggle `[V]`).
- Small office scene.
- Basic interaction.
- Company setup/state.
- Cash and time.
- One software product.
- Simplified product development.
- One employee role (Developer).
- Simplified productivity.
- Simplified market and sales.
- Basic management UI.
- Save/load.

## Excluded
- Multiplayer.
- Advanced NPC AI.
- Complex competitor simulation.
- Multiple industries.
- Multiple offices/floors.
- Advanced building editor.
- Advanced research tree.
- Taxes.
- Stock market.
- Procedural city.
- Hundreds of fully rendered employees.
- Modding.
- Deep feature/version/technical-debt simulation.

## Scope control
Jika fitur tidak membantu membuktikan core loop prototype, pertimbangkan untuk menunda. Perubahan scope dibahas sebelum implementasi.
