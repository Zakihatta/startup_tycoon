# Office System

## Purpose
Ruang fisik perusahaan yang dapat dieksplorasi, diinteraksikan, dan dikembangkan bertahap.

## MVP office
- Small office.
- Floor and walls.
- Door and window.
- Basic lighting.
- Workstation.
- Desk, chair, computer.
- Interactable objects.

## Architecture
Aset modular dengan ukuran/grid konsisten. Office layout data terpisah dari mesh asset dan dapat disimpan.

## Future
Modular construction, furniture placement, rotation/grid snapping, expansion, multiple rooms, office upgrades, decor/comfort, equipment upgrades.

## Interaction
Objek dapat membuka UI, mengubah state, atau mengakses aktivitas. Contoh: workstation membuka product/task screen.

## MVP exclusions
Building editor kompleks, multiple floors, city construction, advanced construction simulation.
