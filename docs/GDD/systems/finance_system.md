# Finance System

## Purpose
Sumber kebenaran transaksi, cashflow, biaya, pendapatan, dan financial risk.

## Financial variables
Cash balance, revenue, expenses, profit/loss, payroll, rent, equipment costs, product costs, marketing costs (future/MVP tuning), loans (future), reports.

## MVP
- Cash balance.
- Rent expense.
- Salary expense.
- Product development cost.
- Basic product sales revenue.
- Profit/loss summary.
- Low-cash warning.

## Transaction principle
Perubahan cash dicatat sebagai transaksi atau event ekonomi yang dapat ditelusuri. Hindari balance berubah tersembunyi dari berbagai systems.

## Future
Loans and interest, taxes, detailed cashflow, forecasting, investor funding, bankruptcy/recovery.

## Risk
Financial failure harus dapat dipahami pemain dan disertai warning/opportunity to respond. Bankruptcy rules TBD.
