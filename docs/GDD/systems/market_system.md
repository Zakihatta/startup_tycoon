# Market System

## Purpose
Mensimulasikan demand, persaingan, trend, positioning dan sales produk.

## Factors (proposed)
- Demand.
- Market size.
- Competition.
- Trends.
- Price sensitivity.
- Product quality.
- Company reputation.
- Product awareness (future).

## MVP simplification
Sales calculation sederhana berdasarkan quality, demand baseline, price (jika digunakan), dan reputation. Formula final perlu ditune; competitor simulation kompleks tidak diperlukan.

## Future
Competitors, segments, advertising, trend cycles, customer feedback, pricing strategy, market share, positioning.

## Feedback
UI/report menjelaskan faktor yang memengaruhi sales secara ringkas dan actionable.
