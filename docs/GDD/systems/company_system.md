# Company System

## Purpose
Menyimpan identitas, status dan progression perusahaan.

## Proposed data
- Company name
- Brand/logo
- Industry (MVP: software/technology)
- Cash balance (authoritative source berada di finance)
- Reputation
- Employees
- Products
- Company progression
- Office/assets

## MVP
- Company name.
- Company status.
- Cash balance terhubung dengan finance.
- Struktur untuk satu product.
- Employee roster.
- Company summary.

## Future
Brand customization, multiple products, multiple offices, divisions, specialization.

## Design notes
Hindari duplikasi data yang dapat dihitung dari sumber data lain. Tetapkan satu sumber kebenaran.
